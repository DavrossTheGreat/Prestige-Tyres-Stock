How it works

Designed for a single premises
1. Choose car tyre, truck tyre or truck rim.
2. Search by size or specification.
3. Confirm brand and pattern / model.
4. Record stock received or booked out.
5. Review history or export inventory.
Before going live: this prototype stores inventory and customer records only in this browser. Re-torque reminders appear in this page when due; they are not push notifications. Real-time sharing between staff requires a secure shared database, staff sign-ins and hosting.
